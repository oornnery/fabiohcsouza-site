## What is kept

**Contact.** Subject, name, e-mail, phone (if given) and the message. Kept for 2 years.

**Newsletter.** The e-mail and language, until you unsubscribe. Unconfirmed subscriptions are deleted after 7 days.

**Visits.** Counted without cookies and without identifying anyone.

**Likes.** Only the total per post, with no record of who liked it.

**IP address.** Used only to block abuse, and deleted within a day.

## Cookies

The site sets one cookie, only to remember the light or dark theme. Cloudflare may set another to check that a visit isn't a bot.

## Who gets the data

Cloudflare, which delivers the site. The data is not sold or passed to anyone else.

## Your data

To see, correct or delete your data, write to [contact@fabiohcsouza.dev](mailto:contact@fabiohcsouza.dev).
