## What I keep

**Contact form.** Subject, name, e-mail, phone (if you give one) and your message. I use them to reply and delete them after 2 years.

**Visits.** I count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and builds no profile of visitors.

**IP address.** It is kept for up to 10 minutes, only to block floods of requests and form posts.

## Cookies

The site sets one cookie, `theme`, when you switch between light and dark. Cloudflare may set another, `cf_clearance`, if it needs to check that a visit isn't a bot.

## Who gets the data

Cloudflare, which all of the site's traffic goes through. The e-mails are sent from my own server. I don't sell or pass data to anyone else.

## Your data

To see, correct or delete what I hold about you, write to [contact@fabiohcsouza.dev](mailto:contact@fabiohcsouza.dev).
