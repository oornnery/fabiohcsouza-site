I've been in telecom since 2018, and at net2phone Brazil (IDT Corporation) since 2020. I configure and maintain SBCs, SIP trunks, SS7 signaling, wholesale routes and carrier interconnects. In networking, I work with P2P links, ISP connections and BGP, and respond to critical incidents. I monitor call quality and troubleshoot with SIP traces and Wireshark captures.

In software development, I work from planning and architecture through to deploy, covering system design, databases, process automation and systems integration. With AI, I integrate LLM agents into those systems and build RAG pipelines, MCP servers and harnesses.

On the infrastructure side, I manage Linux servers, set up firewalls and WAFs, and deploy to AWS with Docker. I also set up monitoring and alerts for those environments and analyze the logs in Splunk.
