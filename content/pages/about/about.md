I've been in telecom since 2018, and at net2phone Brazil (IDT Corporation) since 2020.

I configure and maintain SBCs, SIP trunks, SS7 signaling, wholesale routes and carrier interconnects, and troubleshoot signaling and audio with SIP traces and packet captures. I also work with P2P links, ISP connections and BGP.

I build software from planning to deploy: architecture, system design, databases and full-stack development, plus process automation and systems integration. I integrate LLM agents into those systems and build RAG pipelines, MCP servers and harnesses.

I manage Linux servers, set up firewalls and WAFs, build CI/CD pipelines and deploy with Docker, with tunnels and VPNs between environments. I build and maintain the monitoring, alerts and logs of those environments, and handle critical incident response.
