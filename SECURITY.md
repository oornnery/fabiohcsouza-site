# Security policy

Found a vulnerability in fabiohcsouza.dev or blog.fabiohcsouza.dev? E-mail **contact@fabiohcsouza.dev** with what
you found and how to reproduce it. Please don't open a public issue for it. I'll answer within a few days.

The same address is in `/.well-known/security.txt` on both sites. What the sites do to protect themselves and their
visitors is in [docs/security.md](docs/security.md).
